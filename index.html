WHY THIS GAME?

Every drop matters.

Clean water can change a community. This class project is inspired by charity: water and its mission to help people get access to clean water.

How to play

  1. Tap blue pipes until they form one connected route.
  2. Matching pipes earn +10 points.
  3. Brown pollution costs 15 points and 4 seconds, then disappears.
  4. Finish all three villages to win!

Milestones ⭐

  • Earn points to unlock messages!
